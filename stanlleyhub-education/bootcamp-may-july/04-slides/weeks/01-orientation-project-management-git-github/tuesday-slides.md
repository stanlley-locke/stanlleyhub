# Week 01 — Tuesday Slides

## Slide 1 — Title
Week 01 — Orientation and Project Management Foundations

## Slide 2 — Session Objectives
By the end of this class, students should:
- understand the bootcamp structure
- understand what software engineering is
- understand the software lifecycle
- define MVP
- understand backlog, sprint, and board workflow

## Slide 3 — What this bootcamp is
- practical
- project-based
- GitHub-driven
- live and interactive
- focused on building, not just watching

## Slide 4 — Weekly rhythm
- Tuesday = concept
- Thursday = workshop
- Saturday = review and sprint planning

## Slide 5 — What is software engineering?
- solving real problems using software
- more than just coding
- includes planning, testing, deploying, and maintaining

## Slide 6 — What is a software project?
- an organized effort to build a digital solution
- examples: CRM, e-commerce, inventory app, student manager

## Slide 7 — Software lifecycle
- problem
- requirements
- planning
- design
- development
- testing
- deployment
- maintenance

## Slide 8 — MVP
- minimum viable product
- smallest version that solves the core user problem

## Slide 9 — Scope and priorities
- not every feature belongs in the first version
- beginners must learn to reduce scope

## Slide 10 — Agile basics
- build in small steps
- review often
- improve continuously

## Slide 11 — Core PM terms
- backlog
- sprint
- task
- milestone
- blocker
- retrospective

## Slide 12 — Kanban board
- To Do
- In Progress
- Review
- Done

## Slide 13 — In-class activity
Break a simple app idea into tasks.

## Slide 14 — Assignment preview
Create:
- intro repo
- project board
- project scope note
