# Grading Overview

## What is evaluated
Assignments may be reviewed based on:
- completion
- correctness
- clarity
- documentation
- GitHub hygiene
- effort
- ability to explain the solution

## Common scoring approach
Use simple categories where possible:
- Excellent
- Good
- Needs Improvement
- Incomplete
- Not Submitted

## Important note
The goal of grading in this bootcamp is not just to score work. The goal is to measure understanding, consistency, and practical growth.
