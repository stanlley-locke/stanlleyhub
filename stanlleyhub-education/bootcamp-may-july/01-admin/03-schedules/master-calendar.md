# Master Calendar

## Bootcamp duration
12 weeks

## Active class days each week
- Tuesday
- Thursday
- Saturday

## Weekly rhythm
- Monday: release weekly roadmap
- Tuesday: concept class
- Thursday: workshop class
- Saturday: review/checkpoint/demo class

## Major phases
- Week 0: onboarding
- Weeks 1 to 4: foundations
- Weeks 5 to 9: full-stack and Python application work
- Weeks 10 to 11: AI and DevOps
- Week 12: capstone demo and graduation
