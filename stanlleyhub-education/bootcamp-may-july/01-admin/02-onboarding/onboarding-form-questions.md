# Onboarding Form Questions

## Basic information
1. Full name
2. Preferred name
3. Email address
4. Phone number / WhatsApp number
5. Location / timezone

## Background
6. What is your current level in coding?
7. Have you used GitHub before?
8. Have you used JavaScript before?
9. Have you used Python before?
10. Have you built any projects before?

## Logistics
11. What device are you using?
12. Is your internet connection stable enough for live sessions?
13. Can you attend Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday sessions regularly?
14. Do you have any major schedule limitations?

## Goals
15. Why are you joining this bootcamp?
16. What do you want to build by the end?
17. What is your biggest learning goal?
18. What is your biggest fear or challenge?

## Support
19. Do you need help with setup?
20. Is there anything the instructor should know to help you succeed?
