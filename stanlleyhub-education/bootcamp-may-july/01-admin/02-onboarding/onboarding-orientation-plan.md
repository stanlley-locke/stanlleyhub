# Onboarding Orientation Plan

## Duration
60 to 90 minutes

## Goals
- welcome students
- explain how the bootcamp works
- explain expectations
- explain tools and workflow
- reduce setup confusion
- prepare students for Week 1

## Agenda
1. Welcome and bootcamp mission
2. Program duration and weekly flow
3. Class schedule
4. Tools overview
5. Policies overview
6. Submission process
7. Attendance and certification rules
8. GitHub importance
9. Project management importance
10. Questions and setup support

## Required outcomes
By the end of orientation, students should:
- know where classes happen
- know where assignments are posted
- know how work is submitted
- know how communication works
- know how to prepare for Week 1
