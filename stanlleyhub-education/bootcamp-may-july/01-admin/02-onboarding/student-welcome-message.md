# Student Welcome Message

Hello and welcome to the StanlleyHub Software Bootcamp.

Your seat has been confirmed.

We are excited to have you in this practical 12-week learning experience. This bootcamp is designed to help you build real software projects, strengthen your coding foundations, and grow your confidence through consistent live practice.

### What happens next
1. You will join the official WhatsApp group
2. You will receive access to Google Classroom
3. You will complete the onboarding form
4. You will prepare your tools before orientation
5. You will attend the onboarding/orientation session

### What to expect
- Live classes on Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday
- Practical weekly assignments
- GitHub-based project submissions
- Project tracking and accountability
- A final capstone/demo experience

Please complete all onboarding steps on time so you can start strong.

Welcome again, and get ready to build.
