# Saturday Review Format

## Purpose
Saturday classes are for reinforcement, review, project visibility, and sprint planning.

## Flow
1. Attendance and progress check
2. Short recap of weekly topic
3. Demo/code walkthrough from selected students
4. Common mistakes review
5. Project management check-in
6. Sprint planning for next week
7. Questions

## Student PM check-in questions
- What did you complete?
- What is still pending?
- What blocked you?
- What is your next sprint goal?
