# Struggling Student Support Playbook

## Warning signs
- repeated absence
- missed submissions
- silence in communication channels
- confusion with basic workflow
- visible frustration or withdrawal

## Support steps
1. Reach out privately
2. Ask what is blocking progress
3. Clarify whether issue is technical, schedule-related, or emotional
4. Reduce scope if needed
5. Assign a small recovery task
6. Pair with a more active student if appropriate
7. Follow up within a short time

## Tone
Support should be calm, respectful, practical, and encouraging.
