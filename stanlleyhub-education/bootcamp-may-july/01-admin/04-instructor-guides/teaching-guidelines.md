# Teaching Guidelines

## Core teaching philosophy
Teach for understanding, not only completion.

## Main principles
- keep concepts practical
- explain why before how
- code live when possible
- encourage questions early
- review often
- build in small steps
- treat mistakes as part of learning
- prioritize consistency over perfection

## Class style
- start with recap
- teach the concept clearly
- demonstrate live
- let students practice
- review common mistakes
- close with a clear assignment

## Instructor behavior
- be organized
- be consistent
- be encouraging
- be honest about complexity
- do not shame beginners
- do not rush through foundational concepts
