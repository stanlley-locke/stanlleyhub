# Attendance Policy

## Purpose
Attendance is required because this bootcamp is practical, live, and project-based.

## Minimum attendance requirement
Students must attend at least 75% of all live sessions to remain eligible for a certificate.

## Attendance is tracked by
- live class attendance log
- participation during class
- project checkpoint visibility
- assignment follow-through where needed

## Valid attendance
A student is marked present if they:
- join on time or within the grace period
- stay for the main instructional portion
- participate in the class activity or check-in

## Late arrival
Students arriving late may still be marked present if they join within the first 20 minutes and actively participate.

## Absence process
If a student will miss class, they should notify the instructor or admin before class whenever possible.

## Repeated absence
If a student misses 2 sessions in a short period without communication:
- they should be contacted privately
- their progress should be reviewed
- they may be placed on a recovery plan

## Impact on certification
Students below the minimum attendance requirement are not automatically eligible for certification, even if they complete some assignments.
