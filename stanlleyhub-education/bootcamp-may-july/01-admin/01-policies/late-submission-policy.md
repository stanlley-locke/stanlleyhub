# Late Submission Policy

## Purpose
Deadlines help maintain weekly momentum and fairness.

## General rule
Assignments should be submitted by the stated deadline.

## Grace period
A short grace period may be allowed at the instructor’s discretion.

## Repeated late submissions
Repeated late submissions may affect:
- participation standing
- assignment completion totals
- certificate eligibility

## Communication
If a student anticipates a delay, they should communicate as early as possible.

## Recovery
Students with repeated late work may be placed on a reduced recovery plan.
