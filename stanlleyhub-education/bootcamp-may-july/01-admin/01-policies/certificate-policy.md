# Certificate Policy

## Purpose
Certificates are awarded based on participation, consistency, and completion of required work.

## Basic eligibility
To receive a certificate, a student must:
- attend at least 75% of live sessions
- submit at least 80% of required assignments
- complete the capstone or final project requirement
- remain in good standing under the code of conduct

## Certificate types
Possible certificate types include:
- Certificate of Completion
- Distinction
- Best Capstone
- Most Consistent Student
- Best Collaborator

## Final decision
Certificate issuance is subject to final review by the bootcamp team.

## No automatic certificate
Payment alone does not guarantee a certificate.
