# Communication Policy

## Purpose
Clear communication keeps the bootcamp organized and reduces confusion.

## Main channels
- WhatsApp: announcements, reminders, quick questions
- Google Classroom: official assignment and lesson postings
- Google Meet: live teaching sessions
- GitHub: code submissions and collaboration

## Student expectations
Students should:
- read announcements regularly
- respond respectfully
- ask clear questions
- use the correct platform for the correct purpose

## Urgent communication
Urgent issues should be shared through the fastest available agreed channel.

## Private concerns
Private academic or personal issues should be shared through direct message, not in public group threads.
